1. Sunset Watch: Which June evenings were clearest?

Each tile shows the percentage of nearby coastal land covered by clouds at 7 PM PDT. Lighter gold means less cloud cover; deeper blue means more. Compare dates across the two cities.

2. From Gray to Golden: Does a cloudy morning become a clear evening?

Each dot is one June day. Its horizontal position shows cloud cover at 8 AM; its vertical position shows cloud cover at 7 PM. Dots below the dashed diagonal became clearer by evening, while dots above it became cloudier.

Data and interpretation: NOAA GOES-18 ABI Level-2 Clear Sky Mask, as processed into the accompanying coastal_cloud_june2025.json by the project team. The percentage describes the share of selected nearby coastal land pixels classified as cloudy, not the percentage of the sky visible from a particular viewpoint. Evening cloud cover is a proxy for clear-sky opportunity, not a forecast or measure of sunset color. The 7 PM sample is before sunset in June, and later cloud changes are not observed here. Tooltip categories (0–25%, >25–60%, >60%) are exploratory cloud-cover groupings, not validated sunset-visibility ratings. Only June 2025 is represented.
