Which city is actually cloudier day to day?

Looking at the monthly average does not tell us what happened on each day. At noon, San Francisco was cloudier on more days, but San Diego had a slightly higher average cloud cover of 21.4% compared with 20.7% in San Francisco. That’s because the average depends on how big the differences were, not just which city was cloudier more often.

Press play or move the slider to compare the cities from 6 AM to noon. Each bar represents one day. Blue means San Francisco was cloudier, and orange means San Diego was cloudier. Longer bars show bigger differences. Hover over a day to see the cloud cover for both cities.
