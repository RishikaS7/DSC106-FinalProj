A closer look at June coastal clouds

Daily patterns in San Francisco and San Diego, using hourly coastal cloud-cover observations from June 2025. These two static views show the variation behind monthly averages.

Data: coastal_cloud_june2025.json · June 1–30, 2025 · 720 hourly observations across both cities, 8 AM–7 PM PDT.

The existing project attributes these data to NOAA GOES-18 ABI Level-2 Clear Sky Mask. Percentages describe sampled coastal land, rather than cloud type or sunshine directly. The morning-to-noon comparison does not establish an exact clearing time or a cause.
