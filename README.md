# ☀️ Who Gets the Sun First? ☀️

**An explorable explanation of California's "June Gloom," San Francisco vs. San Diego**

Every June, a deck of low cloud called the *marine layer* parks off the California coast and creeps ashore
overnight. The usual story says it's a Bay Area problem, but San Diegans swear their mornings are just as gray.
We used hourly GOES-18 satellite scans from June 2025 to watch the marine layer move, hour by hour, and to find out
which city actually spends more of June under cloud.

## Data

NOAA **GOES-18 ABI Level-2 Clear Sky Mask** (ABI-L2-ACMC): one scan per hour, June 2025, covering the California coast.
Each pixel is labeled cloudy or clear. We measure "coastal land" inside a box around each city
(SF: 37.60–37.90°N, 122.35–122.55°W; SD: 32.65–33.00°N, 117.10–117.30°W).
